## What we process and why

Through the contact form we process your **name, e-mail address, chosen project type and the text of your message**. The purpose is to reply to your message and agree on next steps. The legal basis is your consent given by submitting the form (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) and, subsequently, steps prior to entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

When the form is submitted, Cloudflare Turnstile processes technical browser data to tell humans from automated bots. Your IP address is used briefly to limit the number of submissions (spam protection) and is not stored permanently.
